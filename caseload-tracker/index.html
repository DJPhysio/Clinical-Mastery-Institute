What's in the tracker

The CMI Caseload Tracker is a straightforward spreadsheet designed for early-career physiotherapists managing a growing caseload in private practice. It gives you a single place to capture your complex or high-concern patients, note your working diagnosis and reasoning, and flag anything you want to revisit before the next session.

It is not a clinical notes replacement. It is a thinking tool — the kind of thing that helps you walk into a Tuesday morning feeling prepared rather than reactive.
