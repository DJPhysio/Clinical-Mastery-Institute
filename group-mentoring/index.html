How it works

Small groups,
real conversations.

Group sessions are structured, not a free-for-all. Each session has a framework: a case or scenario to work through, facilitated discussion, and individual contributions from everyone in the group.

What you get

More than just
another CPD hour.

Group sessions are valuable in a way that 1:1 mentoring cannot replicate. Hearing how another physio would approach a case differently — and why — is one of the most effective ways to stress-test your own reasoning.

You also get normalisation. A lot of what early-career physios struggle with is the assumption that everyone else has it figured out. Group sessions make it obvious that they don't — and that's enormously useful.

  • Structured case-based reasoning sessions
  • Peer discussion and collaborative problem-solving
  • Facilitated by Daniel — not left to run itself
  • Regular cadence builds on previous sessions
  • Community of clinicians at a similar stage
