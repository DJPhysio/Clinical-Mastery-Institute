The problem

What an underdeveloped junior
actually costs you.

The junior physio who is technically capable but quietly struggling costs your clinic in ways that don't always show up immediately on the schedule. Longer treatment episodes. More conservative management out of uncertainty. Referrals that weren't necessary because they didn't feel confident enough to hold the case.

Then there's the human side. A clinician who feels unsupported and isolated is a clinician who is already thinking about leaving. Replacing a physio costs you far more than investing in keeping one.

Supervision helps, but it is usually incidental — a quick word between patients, if there's time. That is not enough for a clinician who is genuinely trying to develop their clinical reasoning in a systematic way.

  • Longer treatment episodes reduce billing efficiency and patient throughput
  • Under-confident clinicians refer on cases they could manage — reducing revenue and patient experience
  • Staff who feel unsupported leave — recruitment and onboarding costs are significant
  • Senior staff burn out when they become the informal support system for the whole team
  • Your clinic's reputation depends on every clinician performing confidently
The solution

Structured mentoring
outside your walls.

What clinic mentoring includes.

CMI works with your junior physios directly — either as individual 1:1 sessions or in a group session where all your junior physios participate together. Sessions happen online via Google Meet, outside clinic hours, so there is no disruption to your schedule.

The mentoring is focused on clinical reasoning, confidence, and the practical realities of private practice — not textbook theory they already know.

After every session, a written summary and action plan is emailed to each physio. As the clinic owner, you also receive a copy of these summaries — so you have visibility into your team's development without the mentoring itself losing confidentiality.

What your clinic gets
from structured mentoring.

Faster clinical development

Clinicians who receive structured mentoring reach clinical independence faster than those relying on incidental supervision alone.

Better staff retention

Physios who feel supported and see a path to growth are significantly less likely to leave. The cost of replacing a physio is high — preventing that departure is not.

Improved billing efficiency

More confident clinical reasoning means more appropriate treatment lengths, better patient outcomes, and a healthier schedule.

Reduced senior burden

Your senior clinicians and owners get time back. They are not the fallback support system for questions that could be addressed in dedicated mentoring sessions.

A culture of development

Investing in your team signals that you take their growth seriously. That matters for morale, reputation, and future recruiting.

CPD documentation

Session logs can be provided on request. The written session summaries emailed after every session can also be used as CPD evidence under AHPRA's peer consultation and reflective practice categories.
