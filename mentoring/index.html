How it works

What a session
actually looks like.

Sessions happen online via Google Meet. You come with what's on your mind — a case you can't stop thinking about, a clinical reasoning gap you keep hitting, a patient interaction that didn't go how you expected, or a broader question about where your career is going.

We work through it together. I don't lecture. I ask questions, challenge your reasoning, and help you build a framework you can use the next time you're in that situation without me.

Between sessions, you can message with quick questions. The goal is for you to feel progressively less dependent on support over time — not more.

60-minute sessions

Each session runs for 60 minutes via Google Meet. Enough time to work through a case properly — not just scratch the surface.

Flexible scheduling

Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly — we agree a cadence at the start that fits your schedule and your goals.

Fully confidential

What you share in sessions stays between us. This is a space to be genuinely honest about what's hard.

Email access between sessions

Got a question between sessions? Send an email. Between-session support is included in every arrangement.

What we cover

Built around
what you need.

There is no fixed curriculum. Sessions are driven by what's actually in front of you. Common areas include:

  • Complex case debrief and differential reasoning
  • Clinical reasoning frameworks for musculoskeletal presentations
  • Managing uncertainty and confident decision-making
  • Difficult patient interactions and communication
  • Managing a caseload under pressure
  • Progressing from competent to confident
  • Career planning and specialisation decisions
  • Navigating clinic culture and professional relationships
  • Postgraduate study — is it right for you, and when?
  • Understanding when and how to refer on

Is this for you?

physios who...

This is for you if

  • You are in your first 4 years of private practice
  • You regularly second-guess your clinical reasoning
  • You feel like you're figuring it out alone
  • You want structured, ongoing support — not one-off advice
  • You are motivated to improve and open to challenge

This is not the right fit if

  • You are looking for a formal supervision arrangement for AHPRA requirements
  • You want someone to give you answers rather than help you find them
  • You are not currently in clinical practice
